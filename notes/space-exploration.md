# 🌌 Space Exploration

Humanity has been reaching for the stars since the first satellite launched in 1957.

## Key Milestones

- **1957** — Sputnik 1, first artificial satellite (USSR)
- **1961** — Yuri Gagarin becomes the first human in space
- **1969** — Apollo 11 lands humans on the Moon
- **1990** — Hubble Space Telescope launched
- **1998** — Construction of the International Space Station begins
- **2021** — James Webb Space Telescope launched
- **2024** — Artemis program aims to return humans to the Moon

## The Scale of Space

- Light travels at ~**300,000 km/s** — yet it takes 8 minutes to reach Earth from the Sun.
- The nearest star system, Alpha Centauri, is **4.37 light-years** away.
- The observable universe is about **93 billion light-years** in diameter.

## Unexplored Frontiers

Space shares something in common with Earth's deep oceans — both are largely **unmapped and mysterious**. We know more about the surface of Mars than the bottom of our own oceans.
