# 🌊 Ocean Facts

The ocean covers about **71% of Earth's surface** and contains 97% of Earth's water.

## Depth & Zones

| Zone | Depth | Notable Feature |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Sunlight Zone | 0–200 m | Most marine life lives here |
| Twilight Zone | 200–1,000 m | Bioluminescent creatures |
| Midnight Zone | 1,000–4,000 m | No sunlight reaches here |
| Abyssal Zone | 4,000–6,000 m | Near-freezing temperatures |
| Hadal Zone | 6,000+ m | Deep ocean trenches |

## Fun Facts

- The deepest point, **Challenger Deep** in the Mariana Trench, is ~11 km below the surface.
- Oceans produce about **50% of Earth's oxygen** via phytoplankton.
- The Pacific Ocean is larger than all of Earth's landmasses combined.
- Sound travels **4–5× faster** in water than in air.
