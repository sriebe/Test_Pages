# 🍕 Cooking Basics

Great cooking is built on a handful of fundamental principles that apply across cuisines.

## The Five Mother Sauces

French cuisine identifies five base sauces from which hundreds of others derive:

1. **Béchamel** — milk + butter + flour (white sauce)
2. **Velouté** — light stock + butter + flour
3. **Espagnole** — brown stock + mirepoix + tomato
4. **Sauce Tomat** — tomatoes + stock
5. **Hollandaise** — butter + egg yolk + acid

## Knife Skills

A sharp knife is safer than a dull one. Master these three cuts:

- **Julienne** — thin matchstick strips
- **Brunoise** — tiny cubes (from julienned strips)
- **Chiffonade** — thin ribbons (great for leafy herbs)

## The Maillard Reaction

Heat + amino acids + sugars = **flavor**. This is the browning reaction responsible for the crust on bread, the sear on a steak, and the color of roasted coffee. Don't skip the sear!

## Seasoning

Salt doesn't just make food salty — it enhances existing flavors and suppresses bitterness. Season in layers throughout cooking, not just at the end.
